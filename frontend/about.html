About Us

Our story

Phumelela has been home to generations of students finding their footing away from home for the first time — sharing kitchens, late-night study sessions, and the small wins that add up to a degree.

What we stand for

  • Succeed — a quiet, secure space that actually supports studying, not just sleeping
  • Achieve — a community of students working toward the same goal, living alongside each other
  • Prosper — affordable, NSFAS-accredited housing that removes one more barrier between you and your degree
